About EV Gate

EV Gate is a deterministic EV viability assessment designed to help users evaluate whether an electric vehicle realistically fits their routine, charging setup, and ownership expectations.

The tool is intentionally conservative. It is designed to reduce poor-fit EV decisions rather than maximise conversions or encourage vehicle purchases.

What EV Gate Does

EV Gate evaluates practical ownership factors such as:

  • Charging practicality
  • Driving patterns
  • Routine predictability
  • Public charging dependency
  • Transport flexibility
  • Ownership expectations

What EV Gate Does Not Do

EV Gate does not provide:

  • Financial advice
  • Vehicle recommendations
  • Sales guidance
  • Investment advice
  • Guarantees of ownership experience

The assessment is intended as a practical guidance tool only.

Assessment Philosophy

EV Gate prioritises operational realism over marketing claims. The engine focuses on ownership friction, charging practicality, and behavioural compatibility rather than theoretical technical capability alone.

The assessment uses deterministic logic rather than AI-generated recommendations.
