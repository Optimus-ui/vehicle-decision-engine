Methodology

EV Gate uses deterministic logic to assess whether EV ownership appears operationally viable based on a user's answers.

Assessment Structure

The assessment evaluates seven core areas:

  1. Overnight parking practicality
  2. Expected charging behaviour
  3. Driving intensity
  4. Routine predictability
  5. Charging friction tolerance
  6. Transport flexibility
  7. Ownership expectations

Deterministic Outcomes

Outcomes are generated using predefined scoring and friction thresholds.

The assessment does not use generative AI to create recommendations or personalised persuasion.

Important Limitations

EV Gate cannot account for every real-world variable.

Factors outside the scope of the assessment may materially affect EV ownership suitability, including:

  • Vehicle-specific range
  • Local charging infrastructure quality
  • Battery condition
  • Insurance costs
  • Electricity pricing
  • Financial circumstances

Purpose of the Tool

The purpose of EV Gate is to encourage realistic consideration of ownership practicality before making a vehicle purchasing decision.
