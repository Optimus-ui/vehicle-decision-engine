Two routes

This page assumes you’ve already decided not to keep the car.

Back to PCP decision

Options

  • Sell to a dealer / car buying service: fastest, simplest, usually lower price.
  • Sell privately: more work, usually higher price.

Rule of thumb

  • If time, stress, or certainty matter most → dealer route.
  • If maximising cash matters and you can handle admin → private route.
